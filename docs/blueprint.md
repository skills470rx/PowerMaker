# **App Name**: Rhythm Canvas

## Core Features:

- Template Selection: Users can browse and select from a variety of pre-designed music visualizer templates.
- Audio Upload: Enables users to upload their audio files (e.g., MP3, WAV) for visualization.
- Image Upload for Visuals: Allows users to upload custom images to serve as backgrounds, logos, or elements within their chosen visualizer template.
- AI Style Recommendation Tool: Analyzes the uploaded audio's characteristics (e.g., tempo, mood, genre) and suggests suitable visualizer templates or styling parameters, providing creative starting points.
- Real-time Preview & Customization: Provides an interactive preview of the visualizer effects applied to the audio and allows for basic customization (e.g., color palettes, effect intensity, text overlays).
- Video Rendering: Initiates the server-side process to generate and combine the audio, images, and visualizer effects into a final video file.
- Video Export & Download: Facilitates downloading the final rendered music visualizer video file in standard formats.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color scheme: Dark to create a vibrant stage for visual effects and content. The chosen palette balances creativity with a sophisticated digital aesthetic.
- Primary color: Electric violet-blue (#634CFF), evoking digital energy, creativity, and modern precision, ensuring visual elements pop against dark backgrounds.
- Background color: Deep anthracite-indigo (#201E2E), a subtle dark tone of the primary hue, providing harmony and depth without distracting from the main content.
- Accent color: Bright cyan-sky blue (#73E0FF), offering a sharp contrast to draw attention to interactive elements and highlight dynamic features.
- Headlines font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), for a modern, techy, and slightly futuristic feel, suitable for an innovative creative tool.
- Body text font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), to complement the headlines with its clear, objective, and highly legible appearance for content and descriptions.
- Use sleek, minimalist, and geometric icons with subtle glow effects to complement the digital and dynamic visual nature of the app.
- A clean and organized workspace with a prominent video preview area, intuitive controls for customization, and clear sections for asset management and template selection.
- Incorporate smooth, fluid transitions and subtle animations for UI elements (e.g., buttons, sliders), and dynamic visual feedback for controls to enhance the creative flow.
